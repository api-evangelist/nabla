Dr. Matt Sakumoto, Chief Clinical Product Officer at Nabla, argues that unclosed charts are a hidden organizational cost that slows billing, reduces operational visibility, and consumes clinician capacity, with primary care physicians potentially spending 1-2 hours daily on documentation. He contends that ambient AI can accelerate chart closure, improve revenue cycle efficiency, and restore clinician time for patient care.
